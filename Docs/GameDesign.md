# Ashfall: 60 Seconds to Midnight (Original IP)

## Core Loop
1. **Scavenge (60s real-time):** Player explores collapsing house district, gathers essential items, rescues family, avoids hazards.
2. **Shelter Survival (turn-based day/night):** Manage resources, relationships, stress, humidity, and toxic fungus.
3. **Expeditions:** Send members out with loadout and risk profile.
4. **Event Resolution:** Random, hidden, and relationship-triggered events branch story.

## Unique Systems
- **Rare Rebellion:** Low trust + high stress can trigger character revolt (1-3% base chance, modified).
- **Family Stress:** Individual and household stress impact decisions, AI behavior, and hallucination events.
- **Humidity + Toxic Fungus:** Shelter environment degrades over time; improper cleaning causes fungal spread, sickness, and psychosis modifiers.
- **Ultra Rare True Ending:** Requires hidden flags from 5 rare events, radio chain completion, and rebellion peacefully resolved.

## Visual Direction
- Stylized-realistic HDRP look: filmic color grading, soft skin shading, rich volumetrics, dust motes.
- Nuclear apocalypse atmosphere: ash skies, distant sirens, shockwave rumble, neon emergency signage.

## Endings
- Starvation, infection collapse, armed conflict, evacuation rescue, bunker integration, rebellion takeover, and **True Dawn** (ultra rare).
